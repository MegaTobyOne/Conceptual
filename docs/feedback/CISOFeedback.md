# CISO Feedback

## Overall impression

The required information appears to be recorded, but the interface makes it difficult to use that information for executive oversight. I need a focused view rather than having to work through a broad feature set.

## Intended workflow

I need to:

1. Understand which roles and teams are accountable for each function and control.
2. Identify gaps and link them to the owners responsible for addressing them.
3. Review analytics and trends over time.
4. Use that information to have focused conversations with subject-matter experts and action owners.
5. Track progress against recommended or agreed actions and milestones.

## Experience and pain points

- The underlying records contain the relevant data, but it is hard to retrieve and interpret for this purpose.
- The current interface requires navigating too many features to reach the information needed for executive oversight.
- A dedicated view centred on trends, accountability, gaps, actions and milestones would better support the role.

## Design implications

- Create an executive oversight view that connects controls and functions to accountable roles and teams.
- Surface gaps alongside their responsible owner, agreed actions and milestone status.
- Make trend and change information prominent enough to support focused management conversations.
- Prioritise a concise role-focused workflow, with broader features available without obscuring the core oversight tasks.
