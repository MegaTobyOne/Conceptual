# Analyst Feedback

## Overall impression

I can access analytics and posture documents, but they are primarily informational. I need a clearer, more actionable view of change over time and a better way to navigate requirements in their domain and section context.

## Intended workflow

To report on changes between reporting periods, I need to:

1. Compare the current year with the previous year.
2. Identify which requirements changed and whether each change is positive or negative.
3. See the reason, commentary and related actions behind each change.
4. Navigate to the relevant requirement in its domain and section context.
5. Produce a report that supports follow-up action as well as informing readers.

## Experience and pain points

- Current analytics do not clearly show the difference between the previous and current year.
- It is difficult to determine which requirements changed, how they changed, and whether the outcome is favourable or adverse.
- Commentary, notes and action history would provide the context needed to turn analytics into an actionable report.
- Finding the requirements workspace took effort. Search eventually located a specific requirement, but a browsable domain-and-section structure would better support exploration.
- Connected View points in the right direction, but currently presents too much detail and feels visually noisy.
- Posture documents and other reporting surfaces provide useful information but do not clearly guide the next action.

## Design implications

- Add a period-over-period posture comparison that highlights changed requirements, direction of change and supporting context.
- Present linked commentary, reasons and actions with each material change.
- Provide guided, hierarchical browsing by domain and section alongside keyword search.
- Introduce an appropriate summary level for Connected View, with detail available on demand.
- Design reports and posture views around decisions and follow-up actions, not only status presentation.
